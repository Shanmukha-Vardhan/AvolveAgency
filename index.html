Terms & Conditions

Qualifies for the 2-day guarantee:

  • Frontend-only websites (React/HTML, no backend logic)
  • Up to 5–7 pages (Home, About, Services, Contact, etc.)
  • Client provides all content upfront — copy, images, logo, brand colors — on Day 1
  • One round of revisions included within the 2-day window
  • Delivered as a live Vercel/Netlify deployment (your domain or a preview link)

Does NOT qualify (excluded from guarantee):

  • Domain registration and third-party hosting setup (depends on registrar timelines)
  • Projects requiring custom backend, database, or auth
  • E-commerce / payment gateway integration
  • Projects where client delays content delivery after kickoff
  • Revisions requested after the 2-day window closes
  • Projects requiring third-party API integrations (WhatsApp, maps, CRMs, etc.)

Clock mechanics:

  • The 2-day timer starts only after full content is received and 50% advance is paid
  • "2 days" = 48 working hours, not calendar days
  • Weekends and public holidays pause the clock
